Enjoy a little tennis in Project Zomboid!

Play 1v1 tennis with a friend, or enjoy wall practice on your own.
Earn a little Fitness and Nimble XP while keeping the rally going.

[h2]Controls[/h2]
To start playing or serve, [b]hold the right mouse button to aim, then left-click[/b].
Once the rally begins, release the right mouse button. You can [b]return the ball with left-click while walking or running[/b].

[b]Your mouse cursor controls where you aim your shot.[/b]

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ppebble/playable-tennis/workshop-images/01-racket-conversion.jpg[/img]
[h1]You MUST switch your Tennis Racket to sports use![/h1]
[b]A regular Tennis Racket cannot be used to play.[/b]
Right-click your Tennis Racket in your inventory and select [b]Switch racket to sports use[/b]. This converts it into a [b]Tennis Racket (Sports)[/b].

[list]
[*][b]Right hand / primary hand:[/b] Tennis Racket (Sports)
[*][b]Left hand / secondary hand:[/b] Tennis Ball — required to start, serve, or restart wall practice
[/list]

You do not need to carry or hold a Tennis Ball to return a shot.

If the conversion option is missing, move the racket from your bag into your main inventory and detach it from any attached hotbar slot first.
A Tennis Racket (Sports) cannot be used for regular attacks.

[h2]Space Requirements[/h2]
[b]1v1 Tennis[/b]
[list]
[*]Minimum: 8 tiles wide × 18 tiles long
[*]Maximum: 10 tiles wide × 20 tiles long
[*]Courts can run north–south or east–west.
[*]The court needs a floor and must be clear of blocking obstacles.
[/list]
Vanilla tennis nets are supported when correctly aligned along the middle of the court.

[h2]Wall Practice for Solo Players[/h2]
No court registration is required.
You need a solid wall and a clear path for the ball. Doors, windows, and fences cannot be used as the practice wall.
A wall just 1 tile wide is enough. To start, stand 1–14 tiles from the wall, measured straight out from its face.

The practice area extends 14 tiles from the wall. The selected wall section is up to 5 tiles wide, and the playing area widens toward the back to a maximum of 9 tiles. The entire area does not have to be empty, but the ball needs a clear path.

[list]
[*]Equip a Tennis Racket (Sports) in your right hand and a Tennis Ball in your left hand.
[*]Aim at the wall by holding the right mouse button, then left-click.
[*]Use your cursor to aim and left-click to return the ball.
[/list]

The rally resets if the ball leaves the practice area, bounces twice, or hits an obstacle along its path.

Moving too far away can end the practice session.
To end it manually, [b]right-click → Playable Tennis → Remove wall practice area[/b].

The top of the screen shows [b]Rally | Best | Depth[/b].
Your best rally record is kept after you end the session.

[h2]1v1 Tennis with a Friend[/h2]
[list]
[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ppebble/playable-tennis/workshop-images/03-court-selection.jpg[/img]
[*]Select [b]right-click → Playable Tennis → Draw / replace my court (rectangle selection)[/b].
[*]Click the first corner and the opposite corner to select the court, using a rectangle selection similar to vanilla farming areas.
[*]Both players must right-click and choose the court's [b]Join[/b] option. It includes the court number, for example [b]Join 1 (tennis)[/b].
[*]The server stands in the [b]Serve Zone[/b], and the receiver stands in the [b]Receive Zone[/b].
[*]Both players need a Tennis Racket (Sports). Once they are in position, the server can begin.
[/list]

[b]The first player to join a new game serves first and keeps the serve for that entire game.[/b]
The serving position alternates left and right after each point. Serve into the diagonally opposite service box. The receiver must let the serve bounce once before returning it.

Only the current game's point score is displayed. Game and set win totals are not tracked.

When the game ends, the winner's name is shown with [b]Win[/b].

[h2]Courts and Saved Scores[/h2]
Each account can own one court.
You cannot create a court that overlaps another player's court.

Putting down your racket, temporarily moving away, or disconnecting preserves the current score. The same participants can return and continue playing.
The game ends when a participant selects [b]Leave court[/b], the court is deleted or replaced, or a winner is decided.

[h2]Exercise Benefits[/h2]
Active rallies grant small amounts of [b]Fitness[/b] and [b]Nimble[/b] XP.
Waiting around or repeatedly swinging at nothing does not grant XP.

The sandbox options [b]Fitness XP multiplier[/b] and [b]Nimble XP multiplier[/b] let you adjust each gain separately.
The default is 1. Set either value to 0 to disable that XP gain.

There is no added exercise fatigue or endurance drain. Normal endurance costs from running and other vanilla actions still apply.
